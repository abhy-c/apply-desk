---
name: grill-me
description: Guide Apply Desk setup and resume story interviews with one precise question at a time and evidence-seeking follow-ups.
---
Ask exactly one question at a time. Give a recommended answer and a brief reason where a recommendation is useful. Wait for the answer, then resolve the next dependent decision. Inspect available code and user-selected files before asking questions they answer.
Invite voice answers for longer resume stories when the host supports voice. Never require voice or claim to record it yourself. Confirm the extracted facts and ask follow-ups about context, ownership, actions, outcomes and supportable metrics. Missing facts do not authorize invention.
Keep private answers in the user's selected workspace, outside plugin/release files. Preserve user intent, including uncertainty. Resume language approvals belong to the review UI; an interview answer is not approval of wording.
For job search, confirm role focus, title variations and exclusions, locations, sources, then each ranking criterion as Required, Preferred or Ignore. Ask about additional criteria. Treat work authorization, sponsorship, salary and geographic restrictions as user choices. Record an explicit Skip choice where optional.
Finish by showing what is complete and exactly what each feature still needs. Remind the user to set up scheduling later. Do not schedule anything without their request.
